StorageSense privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

StorageSense shows you what is using the space in your Photos library. Its whole job is reading your photo library, so it is built to make sure nothing about that library ever leaves your phone. This page says what that means in practice.

What StorageSense collects

Nothing. StorageSense has no account, no sign-in, no server and no network access. The App Store privacy label for StorageSense is Data Not Collected.

Photos access

StorageSense asks iOS for permission to read your Photos library. It uses that access to count items, read their file sizes and types (screenshot, video, Live Photo, burst) and show you thumbnails when you open a category. It does not upload, copy or analyse the content of your photos, and it does not run any face, object or scene recognition. If you grant limited access, only the items you selected are read.

What stays on your device

A small summary of the last scan — how many items and how many bytes each category adds up to, and when the scan ran — is stored on your iPhone so the app can open instantly. Individual photo information is never stored; it is read fresh from Photos each time you open a category. The developer cannot see any of it.

Deleting photos

StorageSense never deletes anything on its own. When you confirm a deletion, the app hands the request to iOS Photos, which moves the items to Recently Deleted, where they stay for 30 days and can be restored from the Photos app.

iCloud calculator

The calculator works only on numbers you type in. StorageSense cannot read your iCloud plan or usage and does not try to.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. StorageSense contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party software development kits.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the cached scan summary. Your photos are untouched. You can withdraw Photos access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Children

StorageSense does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If a future version of StorageSense ever adds a feature that sends data anywhere, this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly described in the app.
