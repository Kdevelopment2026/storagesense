What does StorageSense measure?

Your Photos library only: photos and videos, on this device. iOS does not let any third-party app see the rest of the phone (apps, messages, system), so StorageSense never claims to.

Why does the total look smaller than I expected?

Items that live only in iCloud and have not been downloaded to this iPhone take up no space here, so they count as 0. If iCloud Photos is set to optimise storage on this device, a large part of your library may be in that state.

Where did a deleted photo go?

Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted. Everything you remove in StorageSense sits there for 30 days and can be restored with one tap. StorageSense never deletes anything automatically.

Why are there no selfies or faces?

By design. StorageSense does not look at the content of your photos, so it groups by type and size only. Selfies appear under "Everything else".

The scan takes a while

Very large libraries (tens of thousands of items) can take a minute or two the first time. The result is cached, so the app opens instantly afterwards; tap Re-scan whenever you want fresh numbers.

Photos access is off

Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → StorageSense → Full Access (or a limited selection). Then reopen the app.

Requesting a feature or reporting a bug

Email the address above with your iOS version and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help.
