StorageSense for iPhone

See what's filling your Photos library — before you delete anything.

Screenshots, Live Photos, bursts, oversized videos: StorageSense scans on your phone and explains where the space went, ranked by biggest win. No swiping through 40,000 photos. No account. Nothing leaves your device.

What it does

  • A plain-language breakdown. Your library by category and size, with a sentence explaining each one.
  • Biggest win first. Categories ranked by how much space reviewing them recovers.
  • Review before you delete. Nothing is ever removed automatically; everything you delete goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • iCloud calculator. Type in your plan and usage to see whether freeing space would let you drop a tier.
  • Private by construction. No analytics, no upload, no server. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Honest scope

StorageSense reads your Photos library only. iOS gives no app a view of the whole phone, so we don't pretend to have one.
